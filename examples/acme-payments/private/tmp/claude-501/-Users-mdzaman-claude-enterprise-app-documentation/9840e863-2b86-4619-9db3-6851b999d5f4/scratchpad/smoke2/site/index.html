    Acme Payments

    Acme Payments lets you accept card and bank payments and refund them.

    Task Guide
    Take a payment Accept a payment
    Give money back Refund a payment
    Retry safely Idempotent requests
    Find past payments GET /v1/payments returns payments, newest first

    All requests go to https://api.acme.example and authenticate with a secret API key sent as a bearer token.
